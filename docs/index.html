Countdown app

Count down to the moments that matter.

Data Dawn keeps the dates you care about in one place — a holiday, a birthday, a move, a launch — and shows you exactly how long there is to go. Share a countdown and everyone involved watches the same clock.

Get support Read the privacy policy

Available for Android and iOS. Built on Firebase.

One hero countdown

The nearest moment leads the screen with days, hours, minutes and seconds, so the important date is never buried.

Shared with your people

Invite the people involved in a date, or organise them into circles, so everyone sees the same countdown and the same notes.

Yours across devices

Your countdowns sync through your account, so the phone in your pocket and the tablet on the desk agree on how long is left.

About

Data Dawn is an independent app. It is free to use, and there is no advertising in it. An account is used to sync your countdowns and to let you share them; you can also use the app without one, in which case your countdowns stay on that device only.

The data the app collects, why it is collected and how it is deleted is set out in full in the privacy policy.
