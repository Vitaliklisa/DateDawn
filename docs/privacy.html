Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Data Dawn collects, why, and how you get it back or have it deleted.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Data Dawn ("the app", "we") is an independent countdown application for Android and iOS. This policy applies to the Data Dawn mobile applications distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. The app is operated by the Data Dawn developer, contactable at vhomenko119@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. When you create an account, sign in, or use the app, we may collect:

  • Account details — your email address, and your display name if you provide one.
  • Google account information — if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Content you create — the titles, descriptions, dates and notes of the countdowns you make.
  • Sharing information — the email addresses of people you invite to a countdown, and the membership of any circles you create or join.
  • Notification data — if you enable reminders, the push token needed to deliver them.

We do not collect your contacts, your location, your photos, your files, or any advertising identifier. The app contains no advertising and no third-party advertising SDK.

2. How we use the information

  • to create and secure your account, and to sign you in;
  • to save your countdowns and sync them across your devices;
  • to deliver countdowns you have shared to the people you shared them with, and to let them respond;
  • to send reminders you have asked for;
  • to keep the app working and to investigate faults.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it to anyone.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase, specifically Firebase Authentication (for sign-in) and Cloud Firestore (for countdowns, circles and invitations). Google acts as our data processor for this purpose and stores the data on its infrastructure. Google's own privacy policy applies to that processing: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to your countdown data is enforced by Firestore security rules, which restrict reads and writes to the accounts that own or have been invited to that data.

4. Sharing your information

Information is shared only in these cases:

  • With people you invite. When you share a countdown or add someone to a circle, those people can see that countdown, its details and your display name.
  • With our service providers. Google Firebase, as described above, and Google Sign-In if you use it.
  • Where the law requires it. If we are legally compelled to disclose information.

We do not sell personal information.

5. Signing in with Google

If you sign in with Google, Google provides us with your name, email address and profile picture so that we can create and identify your account. This information is used only for authentication and account-based features, and is not shared with anyone else. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account's security settings.

6. Using the app without an account

You can use Data Dawn without creating an account. In that case your countdowns are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. Sharing and cross-device sync require an account.

7. Notifications

Reminders are optional and off until you turn them on. If you enable them, we use a push token issued by your device to deliver reminders about your own countdowns. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app's settings or in your device settings; doing so stops reminder delivery immediately.

8. How long we keep data, and how to delete it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account from inside the app (Settings → Delete account). Deleting your account permanently removes your account record, your countdowns, your notes, your circle memberships and your pending invitations from our systems. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by emailing vhomenko119@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We will action the request within 30 days. See the support page for the exact steps and the information to include.

Note that deleting a shared countdown removes it for everyone it was shared with.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, email vhomenko119@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children's privacy

Data Dawn is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access rules enforced at the database layer. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials private.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
