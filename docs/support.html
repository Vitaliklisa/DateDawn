Support

Help with Data Dawn, account deletion, and data requests.

Contact us

Email vhomenko119@gmail.com and we will get back to you. We aim to reply within 3 working days.

Please include the email address on your Data Dawn account, your device model, and what happened — it saves a round trip.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account and everything in it directly from the app:

  • Open Data Dawn and go to Settings.
  • Scroll to the bottom and choose Delete account.
  • Confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email vhomenko119@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request", sent from the email address on the account. We will delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted: your account record, your countdowns and their notes, your circle memberships, and any pending invitations you sent. If you shared a countdown with other people, deleting it removes it for them too.

Request a copy of your data

Email vhomenko119@gmail.com with the subject "Data request" from your account address. We will send you the personal information we hold, in a readable format, within 30 days.

Common questions

I can't sign in

Check the app has a network connection — sign-in and sync need one, and the app works offline only for countdowns already on the device. If you signed up with email and password, use Forgot password on the sign-in screen to reset it. If you used Google Sign-In, make sure the Google account you are choosing is the one you originally signed up with.

My countdowns aren't showing on another device

Countdowns sync through your account, so both devices must be signed in to the same account. Pull down on the home screen to refresh. If you used the app without signing in, those countdowns live only on the device where you made them.

Notifications aren't arriving

Check that notifications are enabled for Data Dawn in your device's settings (Android: Settings → Apps → Data Dawn → Notifications; iOS: Settings → Notifications → Data Dawn), and that reminders are switched on for that countdown inside the app.

I was invited to a countdown but don't see it

Invitations are sent to an email address. Sign in with the account that uses that email address, then check the invitations inbox in the app.

How do I turn off the app's access to my Google account?

Visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Data Dawn. Note that this signs you out of the app; if you want your data deleted as well, use the account deletion steps above.

Report a problem or an abuse concern

Email vhomenko119@gmail.com with the subject "Report". If you are reporting content shared with you by another user, include the countdown name and, if you can, the email address of the person who shared it, so we can locate it.
