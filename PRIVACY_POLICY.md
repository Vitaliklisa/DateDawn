# Privacy Policy

> **This file is the source text.** The version that Google Play and the App
> Store actually review is the published page:
> `https://vitaliklisa.github.io/DateDawn/privacy.html`
> (source: `docs/privacy.html`). Edit that page — not this file — and keep the
> two in step if you change either.

This privacy policy explains how Data Dawn collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the app.

Data Dawn is operated by the Data Dawn developer (`vhomenko119@gmail.com`). This policy applies to the Data Dawn mobile applications on Google Play and the Apple App Store, and to the Data Dawn web app.

## 1. Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you create an account, sign in, or use the app:

- Name and email address
- Google account information when you sign in with Google
- Event details you create or share in the app
- App activity data, such as event creation and participation records
- Device information needed for app operation and support
- Notification preferences and push token information if notifications are enabled

## 2. How we use the information

We use the information to:

- provide account access and user authentication
- save and sync countdown events and shared event data
- allow collaboration with other users and circles
- send notifications and reminders related to your app activity
- improve app functionality, reliability, and support

## 3. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user information. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 4. Sharing of information

We may share limited information with third-party service providers that help us operate the app, including:

- Firebase (authentication, database, analytics, notifications)
- Google Sign-In services
- App distribution and analytics services used by the project

We do not sell personal information.

## 5. User accounts and Google Sign-In

If you sign in using Google, Google may provide us with profile information such as your name and email address as part of the sign-in process. We use this only for authentication and account-based app functionality.

## 6. Event and shared data

The app may store event information, participant data, shared circle membership, reminder data, and invitation data. This information is required for countdown scheduling, event collaboration, and sharing features.

## 7. Notifications

If notifications are enabled, we may use device tokens and event-related content to send reminders, updates, or follow-up messages relevant to your activity inside the app.

## 8. Your choices

You may:

- sign out of the app at any time
- disable notifications in your device settings
- delete or modify your account-related data according to the app’s account controls
- request information about your stored data by contacting the developer

## 9. Children's privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

## 10. Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected in the app or in the published policy URL.
